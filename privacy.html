Privacy policy

rclone-minipc-backup is a personal application used only by its owner.

  • The only user is the owner. No one else's data is accessed.
  • No data is collected, stored, or shared by this application or its owner with any third party.
  • Google Drive access is limited to files created by rclone itself (the drive.file scope).
  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
